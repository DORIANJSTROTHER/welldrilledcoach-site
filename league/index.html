Questions

For
directors.

Who can use the league key?

The coaches, assistant coaches and program staff of the organization named at purchase. Please don't post the key publicly or share it with other organizations.

We have more than 40 coaches.

Each additional group of 40 coaches needs another license. Email us and we'll put it on one invoice.

Does it cover spring, summer or AAU sessions too?

Yes. The license runs 12 months from purchase, across every session your organization runs in that time.

Do coaches need accounts?

No. Coaches open the link, enter the key and start. Progress saves on each coach's own device. There are no logins and no sync.

Can we pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Email hello@welldrilledcoach.com with your organization name and billing contact, and we'll send an invoice and a W-9.

What's not included?

Custom content, league branding, coach accounts, data sync, live training and phone support.
