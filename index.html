Questions

Good
to know.

Do I need basketball experience?

No. Each practice is laid out block by block, every drill has a demo and cues, and Coach basics covers your first practice minute by minute.

What do I need to run it?

A phone, tablet or computer with Safari or Chrome. You need internet to open the app. There's nothing to install and no account to create.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Progress and custom plans save in the browser on each device. There's no automatic sync, but you can export and import a backup.

Which ages is it for?

Ages 7–11, in two age bands: 7–9 and 10–11. Drills include variations for each band.

Our league wants every coach to have it. Can we get an invoice?

Yes. The League Pilot covers up to 40 coaches for 12 months. Email hello@welldrilledcoach.com for an invoice or a W-9.

What if it isn't right for us?

Email us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
